写作模板

① 现象解释型
Nowadays, ______ has become a common phenomenon on campus. According to a recent survey, ______. Several factors contribute to this trend. First and foremost, ______. What's more, ______. Last but not least, ______. In my opinion, ______. Only in this way can we ______.
适用：校园现象、社会趋势、某行为流行
② 观点选择型
When it comes to ______, opinions vary from person to person. Some believe that ______, while others argue that ______. As far as I am concerned, ______. The reasons are as follows. To begin with, ______. In addition, ______. Taking all these factors into consideration, we may safely conclude that ______.
适用：是否应该、利弊比较、二选一
③ 图表 / 应用型
As is vividly shown in the chart, the number of ______ increased / decreased from ______ to ______. There are several reasons accounting for this change. First, ______. Second, ______. In conclusion, ______. It is high time that we ______.
适用：图表题、倡议书、通知
万能句（各背 3 句就够）：
It is universally acknowledged that ... There is no denying that ... From my perspective, ... It is high time that we took effective measures to ...

技巧速查

听力：视听一致；转折词（but/however/actually）后出答案；同义替换是正确答案；听力结束立刻收答题卡1，必须边听边涂。 阅读：先看题干圈关键词 → 定位原文 → 同义替换比对；排除"偷换概念 / 以偏概全 / 无中生有 / 绝对化"。 写作：3 段，120–180 词，开头结尾背熟，中间两个理由 + 一个例子。 翻译：拆句 → 找主谓宾 → 逐句翻 → 检查时态和单复数；不会的词用解释法绕过去。
